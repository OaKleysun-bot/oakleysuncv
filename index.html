01Individual project · SIEN5001

REA Group

Strategic Analysis

An assessment of competitive advantage as AI reshapes property discovery.

Competitive strategy · Platform economics+

Project Overview

This project evaluated REA Group’s position in Australia’s digital property market using PESTE, Porter’s Five Forces, value chain analysis and VRIO. It examined audience scale, network effects and proprietary data alongside competition, pricing pressure, AI disruption and data governance. The report proposed a two-year Trusted Property Intelligence Ecosystem strategy combining verified-data AI services, transparent agent value reporting and controlled external AI partnerships.

My Role & Contributions

As the sole author, I completed the research, analysis and recommendations. 02Group project · INFS3600

AI Companion Safety

Research & System Design

A proposed governance framework and safety system for AI companion platforms.

Responsible AI · Research methodology+

Project Overview

This project examined psychological and self-harm risks associated with AI companion chatbots for young male users. The team explored engagement-driven design, emotional dependency and inconsistent safety responses, and proposed a duty-of-care compliance framework supported by a Safety Compliance and Escalation System (SCES). The concept combined cumulative risk detection, proportionate interventions, human review and audit records.

My Role & Contributions

I established the project’s foundation by defining the problem, developing the user scenario and selecting the research methodology. I also analysed stakeholder impacts and contributed to the testing process. In addition, I assisted with the prototype presentation to help communicate how the proposed safety system would operate.

04Group project · INFS3040

Lextel

Enterprise Software Evaluation

An evidence-based comparison of three eCommerce platforms for a legacy system replacement.

Vendor evaluation · Digital transformation+

Project Overview

This project evaluated replacement options for Lextel’s legacy WebShop, which faced slow pricing and product updates, fragmented integrations and dependence on technical support. The team compared BigCommerce, SAP Commerce Cloud and Salesforce Commerce Cloud using a weighted framework covering business requirements, extensibility, integration and operational considerations, recommending SAP Commerce Cloud within the case assessment.

My Role & Contributions

I researched the profiles of the three selected vendors—BigCommerce, SAP Commerce Cloud and Salesforce Commerce Cloud. I developed the evaluation matrix for all three vendors and conducted their evidence-based evaluation. I assessed functional fit, extensibility and integration trade-offs against Lextel’s specific requirements to support the team’s final recommendation.
